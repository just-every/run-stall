# Stall target product contract

## Product target

Target approved scope: **16 October 2026**. First buyer: a US small retailer or maker selling physical goods in **USD** through an owned branded online store.

Stall replaces the online-store job sold by **Shopify, Squarespace Commerce, BigCommerce and WooCommerce**: build and merchandise a store, attract and convert shoppers, take payment, deliver orders, support returns and account for the result. **Medusa, Saleor and Fourthwall** are additional switching/extension reference points. This is the product being built towards, not a description of the current release. No homepage, UI or running behavior changes are authorized by this document round.

The core records are stores/themes/pages, collections/products/variants/media/custom fields, stock modes/store-managed quantities/stock-provider mappings/holds, carts/quotes, customers/consent/storefront event preferences, promotions/gift balances, tax/delivery quotes, orders/payments, shipments/returns/exchanges/refunds, messages, channel listings, accounting postings and attributable work history. Every record has an explicit store, identity, revision/state and permission boundary appropriate to its job. Historical imports retain provenance and never become current payment or stock authority.

A single switching journey runs from migration and domain cutover through catalog discovery, checkout, fulfillment, service, return and reconciled books. The clauses specify buyer behavior and the public-door evidence needed to call it complete. Provider test environments/network-edge fakes establish local logic only; live account, domain, carrier, wallet-device and hosted delivery acceptance must be recorded separately. Fixtures are test-only; production has no invented stock, orders, provider success or fallback service.

## Target clauses

### Runtime and store experience

A storefront password hides the shop before launch or during migration staging while staff preview keeps working. The merchant connects an owned domain with verified DNS, TLS and canonical redirects. Published themes serve resized, cached images, and the reference theme is measured against Google's "good" Core Web Vitals thresholds at phone width rather than assumed fast.

Export the theme source and assets, re-import them into an empty store and compare the rendered pages; import a damaged archive and verify a named refusal. Enable the storefront password and confirm public pages, sitemap and feeds are withheld while staff preview works. Follow HTTPS and www/canonical redirects, and record a Core Web Vitals run of the reference theme at 390 pixels.

Results, counts, empty states and shareable URLs agree with published, sellable catalog data; unpublished products stay private. Add a synonym and a pinned product for a query and verify both change results; search a term with no results and find it in the search report.

- **STORE-003 — Content, blog, contact, newsletter and search visibility.** Merchants manage content pages, blog posts, policies, menus, product/collection SEO titles and descriptions, image alt text, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots policy, social previews, structured product data and permanent redirects. Draft/scheduled content is previewable and changes retain publication history. Moving a product URL preserves discovery through redirects. Every theme includes a contact page whose form delivers each submission, with optional order number, to the store's notification email (reply-to set to the shopper) and to a desk inbox, or to a connected help desk such as sibling [HappyToHelp](https://github.com/just-every/run-happytohelp); Cloudflare Turnstile, a honeypot and per-sender rate limits hold spam for review instead of silently dropping it. A newsletter signup block (footer, page section or popup) records an explicit marketing opt-in under CUSTOMER-002 with source, form, page and timestamp, optionally confirmed by double opt-in, and propagates the subscriber to Mango, Klaviyo or Mailchimp through MARKET-001 without duplicating an existing contact.

### Catalog and stock

- **CATALOG-001 — Products, variants and media.** The desk maintains products, handles, SKUs, variant options/combinations, USD prices and compare-at prices, cost basis, taxable categories, categories/tags/attributes, collections, image galleries and video/alt text. Merchants define typed custom fields (metafields such as size charts, care instructions or ingredients) that themes render on product pages and that imports and exports carry. Products can ask the shopper for personalization (engraving or monogram text with a character limit, a choice list, or a file upload with type and size limits), optionally with a USD price add-on; it is validated before add to cart and carried to the cart, checkout review, order, packing slip, confirmation email and exports, and uploaded files are stored privately under erasure (ERASURE-001). Each variant has independent stock mode and availability (STOCK-001/003), shipping weight/dimensions, and customs description, HS tariff code and country of origin for international labels (SHIP-002). Draft, scheduled, published and archived states preserve historical purchased descriptions/prices and expose only approved current listings.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Create a product with size/color variants, multiple images, cost, weight, tax category, HS code, country of origin and a size-chart custom field; publish an approved revision and verify the custom field renders on the product page. Buy one variant, change its current price and archive another; verify gallery/selection and immutable order snapshots through public product and order views. Add an engraving field (20 characters, +$5) and an artwork upload to a product, refuse over-length text and a disallowed file type, buy with valid personalization and verify it with the add-on price on the cart, order, packing slip and confirmation email.

- **CATALOG-002 — Bulk catalog operations and portable files.** Merchants import, export and bulk-edit products/variants, collections, prices, publication, tax settings, stock mode, on-hand quantities per location, weight, dimensions, HS codes, countries of origin and custom fields. Quantity changes from the bulk grid or an inventory CSV are written to each variant's stock authority (STOCK-001/003) with Stall and provider references recorded together. A dry run shows creations, changes, conflicts and rejected rows before an exact reviewed apply; jobs resume without duplicates and offer undo only where later native edits or orders will be preserved. Original source custody and row-level outcomes remain inspectable.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a mixed valid/invalid catalog file, inspect a read-only preview, apply the reviewed rows, resume an interrupted batch, export them and undo untouched changes. Edit/purchase an imported SKU first and verify undo reports the protected row rather than overwriting it. Bulk-edit on-hand quantities in the grid and by an inventory CSV, once with store-managed stock and once with Pier connected, and verify each change appears once in the stock authority's history with a matching reference.

- **STOCK-001 — Live availability through Pier or another stock system.** Stall displays variant-level sellable availability from each variant's stock authority and reserves against the selected location/fulfillment policy. A variant may be untracked or use store-managed quantities with no stock system at all (STOCK-003); Pier is an optional first-class adapter, and a documented connector lets a merchant retain another stock system. Stall owns storefront merchandising and retail order experience; Pier owns the Cin7/inFlow-style inventory, purchasing and wholesale order system. Backorder/preorder rules, lead times, safety stock and stale-provider behavior are visible before purchase. Changes that start in Stall (on-hand adjustments from the product editor, bulk editor or inventory CSV, sale reservations, cancellation releases and accepted restocks from RETURN-002) are written to the connected stock system with Stall's and the provider's references recorded together; changes that start in the stock system (receipts, counts, transfers) flow back to availability. Neither side becomes a second warehouse ledger. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Connect Pier, publish variants across two locations, make an external stock change and verify public availability. Adjust a variant's on-hand quantity from the product editor, the bulk editor and an inventory CSV and verify each adjustment lands once in Pier with matching references. Repeat both via the documented alternate adapter; test backorder refusal/allowed lead time, stale stock and duplicate reservation events with matching stock-provider references. Set a safety stock of 2 on a variant with 5 on hand and verify the storefront sells exactly 3 before showing it sold out.

- **STOCK-002 — Expiring checkout holds and stock race resolution.** Checkout rechecks availability and creates a bounded stock hold before payment commitment. Holds expire or release after abandonment, failed payment and cancellation; a verified payment converts the same hold into a reservation once. Late payment or shortfall remains an explicit exception with feasible fulfillment, shopper-approved substitution or reconciled cancellation/refund rather than silently overselling.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Two shoppers attempt the last unit through checkout. Expire one hold and fail another payment; verify releases. Deliver late/duplicate success and lose a reservation response; confirm one reservation or a visible paid-shortfall case resolved by the shopper-approved outcome. While the first shopper's hold is live, verify the second shopper is told the last unit is held, with the hold's remaining time, and cannot pay for it, and that it becomes buyable again as soon as the hold expires or that payment fails.

- **STOCK-003 — Store-managed quantities and untracked items without a stock system.** Each variant has a stock mode: untracked (made-to-order or unlimited, optionally with a production lead time shown before purchase), store-managed quantities kept by Stall per store location, or a connected stock system (STOCK-001). With no stock system connected, a merchant can sell, hold, reserve and fulfill untracked and store-managed variants; adjust on-hand counts with a reason (received, correction, damaged, cycle count) from the product editor, the bulk editor or an inventory CSV; see on-hand, committed and available quantities per location with adjustment history; and choose per variant whether to continue selling when out of stock. Store-managed counts are the retail quantities a Shopify-class store keeps, not purchasing, transfers or wholesale, which remain Pier's job. Connecting Pier or another stock system later moves opening balances through a reviewed handoff, after which that system is authoritative and Stall's counts become read-through. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

  **Acceptance evidence:** With no stock system connected, sell and manually fulfill (ORDER-002) a made-to-order untracked variant and a store-managed variant; verify committed and on-hand counts and that the last unit cannot be oversold unless continue-selling is on. Adjust on-hand counts from the editor, bulk editor and CSV with reasons and inspect the adjustment history. Connect Pier, review and apply the opening-balance handoff, and verify later adjustments are written through Pier with matching references while Stall keeps no independent count.

### Cart, payment and commercial totals

- **CART-001 — Persistent cart and guest or account checkout.** Shoppers add/remove variants, change quantities, retain a cart across refresh/sign-in, and check out as guests or account holders. Native shopping/checkout steps collect US addresses, and international addresses in the destination's format for zones the merchant serves (SHIP-001), plus contact, selected delivery method, an optional order note and a gift message with an optional gift receipt that hides prices. They show reviewed USD item, discount, gift-card, shipping, tax and final payable totals before committing payment. Material changes require review again; stock, price and address errors preserve the basket. Saved carts never grant access to another customer. Order notes and gift messages appear on the order, packing slip and confirmation. Merchants can add their own checkout fields (for example "How did you hear about us?", a preferred delivery date or a gift-wrap choice) as required or optional text, choice or date inputs; answers are stored on the order and appear in the order desk, exports and reports (ANALYTICS-001).

  **Acceptance evidence:** Build a multi-variant cart, reload and sign in, choose a US address and delivery method, add an order note and gift message, apply a discount/gift card and review exact totals. Change price or availability concurrently and verify a fresh review; repeat as a guest, as a different account and with a Canadian address. Verify the note and gift message on the order and packing slip and a gift receipt without prices. Add a required "How did you hear about us?" choice field, verify checkout refuses to continue without it, and find the answer on the order, in the order export and in a report grouped by answer.

- **PAY-001 — US dollar cards, wallets, PayPal, pay-later and manual payments.** The merchant connects their own Stripe and PayPal accounts. Checkout supports USD cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link/Shop-style accelerated wallet checkout, PayPal and PayPal Pay Later, and buy-now-pay-later through Stripe's Klarna, Affirm and Afterpay methods, using supported provider adapters and eligibility checks. Pay-later options appear only within each provider's current order-amount and shopper-country eligibility, show the provider's installment terms, and can be advertised on product and cart pages through provider messaging; the merchant is paid by the provider and refunds return through the same method. Merchants can also offer manual methods (bank transfer, cash on delivery, pay at pickup) whose orders stay pending payment until staff record receipt; fulfilling an unpaid manual order requires an explicit merchant setting. Eligible payment choices and unsupported devices are honestly presented; customer payment credentials stay with the processor and wallet. Refunds and receipts retain original method/currency references. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Complete USD purchases with a card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and PayPal through their supported test/eligible-device doors; inspect provider IDs, amount/currency, failure/cancel paths and a same-method refund. Complete a test-mode Klarna, Affirm, Afterpay and PayPal Pay Later purchase and refund each; verify every pay-later option is withheld, with an explanation, below and above its eligibility range. Verify provider pay-later messaging appears on the product and cart pages with installments computed from the current price, updates when the cart changes, and disappears outside eligibility. Place bank-transfer and pay-at-pickup orders and verify they stay unpaid and unfulfillable until staff record payment. A live provider/device acceptance run is required before claiming production wallet readiness.

- **PAY-002 — Authorization, capture, cancellation and disputes.** Merchants choose supported immediate capture or authorize-then-capture rules, see pending/authorized/captured/failed/expired/canceled states, and reconcile asynchronous payments, voids, disputes, chargebacks and provider fees. No fulfillment occurs on an unverified browser return or mere authorization. Expired authorization and a disputed payment create accountable staff work. For a dispute the desk assembles evidence (order and receipt, policies, customer communications, carrier tracking and delivery scans) into the processor's evidence fields; staff review and submit it to Stripe or PayPal before the response deadline, and the outcome and dispute fees reconcile to the order. Permitted captures/refunds bind current order and provider state. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize an order without capture, attempt fulfillment, capture once, then process a failed/expired authorization, asynchronous success and dispute event. Assemble and submit evidence for a test dispute on Stripe and PayPal, and verify the processor received it and the won/lost outcome and fee reconcile. Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks must preserve actual processor state, correct balance and a visible owner action.

- **PAY-003 — Verified payment and duplicate-safe paid reservation.** A signed payment event must match its raw body, timestamp, event identity, original order/session, exact-cent amount and currency, and canonical processor state before an order is treated as paid. Repeated delivery and lost stock responses reconcile to the same paid order and Pier reservation. A paid shortfall remains recorded; a browser success page alone proves nothing. Changed reuse of an idempotency identity is refused.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Through public checkout and the signed webhook send unpaid, stale, wrongly signed, changed-total and duplicate events; lose the Pier response after its commit, then reconcile. Inspect one payment/order/reservation and durable paid-before-reservation history.

- **PROMO-001 — Discounts, automatic promotions and controlled budgets.** Merchants create each Shopify-class discount type as either a code or an automatic discount: percentage or fixed amount off selected products, variants or collections; percentage or fixed amount off the order subtotal; Buy X get Y (buy a quantity of, or spend an amount on, qualifying items and get specified items free or at a percentage/amount off, with a per-order use limit, including buy-one-get-one); and free shipping with a minimum subtotal or quantity, eligible zones/countries and an optional maximum rate. Each has date windows, customer eligibility/segments, minimums, per-customer/total usage, combination rules across product, order and shipping discounts, and budgets. Merchants can bulk-generate unique single-use codes for influencers or affiliates, export them and see redemption per code. Checkout explains applied/refused promotions, allocates discounts to lines for tax and refunds, records the chosen rule revision and emits qualification/redemption/reversal events. Concurrent baskets cannot exceed a shared cap; refund/cancellation treatment is explicit.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Configure each type once as a code and once as an automatic discount: amount and percentage off a product and a collection, amount off the order, a buy-one-get-one and a spend-$X Buy X get Y, and free shipping over $50. Build qualifying and non-qualifying baskets and verify the reviewed checkout totals, line allocations and tax to the cent. Exercise combination rules, race the last redemption of a capped code, generate a batch of unique single-use codes and refuse a second use of one, cancel one order and partially refund another. Reconcile campaign usage, budget and exact order/tax totals from the merchant report and emitted events.

- **PROMO-002 — Gift cards and stored redemption balances.** Merchants sell or issue gift cards, deliver secure codes, inspect a balance ledger and redeem partially across orders alongside another method. Concurrent redemption, cancellation and refund preserve value exactly once; issuance, expiry restrictions and cash-out handling follow the configured US jurisdiction/policy and are explicit to the shopper. Gift cards sold to shoppers follow the federal CARD Act (no expiry earlier than five years after issue or last load, no dormancy or inactivity fee in the first twelve months) unless the store's state is stricter, and the store's state sets cash-out rules, such as California's requirement to pay balances under $10 in cash on request; free promotional cards are labeled as promotional with their own terms. Staff can also issue store credit to a customer outside any return (goodwill, loyalty or a price adjustment) with a USD amount, reason and optional expiry; it shows in the customer's account and applies at checkout like a balance, alone or alongside another method, and return-resolution credit (RETURN-002) uses the same ledger of issue, use, expiry and staff adjustment. Exported balances and migrated cards reconcile without revealing full codes to unauthorized staff.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a USD gift card, deliver and redeem it partly, race its remaining balance in two checkouts, and cancel/refund the winning order. Inspect the balance ledger, shopper receipt, staff permissions and reconciled migrated opening balance. Try to give a sold card a two-year expiry and verify refusal under the five-year rule, while a promotional card may carry it. With a California store, request cash-out of a $7.50 remaining balance and verify a recorded payout zeroes the ledger, and verify a $15.00 balance is refused cash-out. Issue $20.00 goodwill store credit to a customer with no return, sign in as that customer, see it in the account and apply $12.00 of it at checkout alongside a card; verify the ledger shows $8.00 remaining. Sell a $50.00 gift card through storefront checkout, verify the buyer receives its code by email once payment is verified, and redeem it at checkout on a later order.

- **TAX-001 — US sales tax and exemptions.** Stripe Tax, Avalara AvaTax or TaxJar calculates US state/local sales tax using the merchant's configured registrations, ship-from/destination, product tax categories, exemption evidence and shipping/discount treatment. Checkout shows jurisdictional tax before payment; order records preserve the calculation reference and refunds reverse the correct tax. Provider outage or incomplete configuration blocks a taxed sale with a useful explanation, rather than inventing a zero rate. A sales-tax liability report shows, for any filing period, a state overview (gross, taxable, non-taxable and exempt sales, shipping, tax collected, refunds and adjustments, with marketplace-facilitated channel sales separated), then county, city and special-district breakdowns, then the contributing transactions; each level exports as CSV and drills to orders and provider calculation/transaction references, and refunds land in the period they occur. A nexus view tracks per-state sales and transaction counts against the merchant's configured economic-nexus thresholds and warns before one is crossed. Registration and filing stay with the merchant, adviser or a filing service such as Avalara Returns or TaxJar AutoFile, fed by these exports.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Configure two US destinations, taxable/exempt goods, a documented exempt customer and discounted shipping. Check provider-backed totals before payment and a partial-return tax adjustment; interrupt the tax provider and verify checkout refuses an unpriced obligation and reports the cause. After taxed sales to two states (one with county, city and special-district tax) and a partial refund in the following month, run the liability report for each month and verify the state, jurisdiction and transaction levels and their CSVs reconcile exactly to the order records and tax-provider transaction references, with the refund in its own period. Approach a configured nexus threshold and verify the warning. Quote two US addresses in the same ZIP code that fall in different city or special-district jurisdictions and verify each is charged its own address-level rate.

- **RISK-001 — Fraud indicators and human review.** Processor fraud signals, address/payment mismatches and order risk appear in a restricted review queue with their provenance. Merchant-configured rules hold risky fulfillment and notify authorized staff; an explicit release/cancel decision records the reviewer. Scores are advisory and never silently assert fraud, capture funds or deny a refund without the configured authority.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Create low-risk and flagged provider test orders, inspect the restricted queue, attempt fulfillment while held and release/cancel with an authorized reviewer. Repeated risk callbacks must not duplicate holds or messages.

### Fulfillment, orders and returns

- **SHIP-001 — Shipping zones, live rates, international USD shipping, pickup and delivery.** Merchants configure shipping zones for the US (by state/ZIP) and for Canada and other countries served in USD from the same store, with service eligibility, free/flat/weight-based rules, handling cutoffs and delivery promises; checkout rates packages from current variant weight/dimensions through ShipStation, Shippo or EasyPost for USPS, UPS and FedEx domestic and international services. International rates state that duties and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility unless the merchant enables a supported duties-paid (DDP) landed-cost service through the shipping provider, and checkout collects the destination's address format and the phone number carriers require. Destinations outside configured zones and embargoed destinations are refused before payment. Shoppers choose eligible service, local pickup at a named store location (shown with its address, opening hours, pickup instructions and expected ready time) or local delivery inside the merchant's delivery area (set per location by ZIP codes or a radius, with its own fee, minimum order and delivery instructions) with clear price and instructions; changed address/package invalidates stale quotes. Pickup and local-delivery orders are completed through SHIP-007, never through a carrier label. Each location has a daily handling cutoff, handling days and a business-day and holiday calendar; the delivery date or range promised at checkout is computed from them and the service's transit time, is shown on the product page and in checkout, and the paid order keeps the promise it was sold under. Split-source orders show combined charges and feasible dates. Which rates and services apply to which products is set by shipping profiles (SHIP-004); this clause's zones and rules are the general profile every product starts in. Local currencies and regional storefronts are EXPAND-001. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Quote different US ZIPs and package weights with live provider test rates, select pickup/local delivery, change address after selection and verify repricing. Select local pickup and verify the location, hours and instructions are shown and kept on the paid order; enter a ZIP outside the delivery area and verify local delivery is not offered, and below the delivery minimum verify checkout states the amount still needed. Set a 2 p.m. Eastern handling cutoff with one handling day and a holiday the next business day; place orders just before and just after the cutoff and verify promised delivery ranges one business day apart that skip the holiday, kept on each paid order. Quote Canadian and UK addresses in USD with provider rates and verify the duties disclosure. Refuse destinations outside configured zones and show actionable carrier outages; the paid order must retain the selected service and quoted charge. At checkout for a US address verify that live USPS and UPS services are both offered at the provider's current rates, and that a rate changed in the provider test account is reflected in the next quote.

- **SHIP-002 — Purchase, print and void carrier labels.** Authorized staff buy and print outbound/return labels through ShipStation, Shippo or EasyPost, prepare manifests and packing slips, and void unused labels with reconciled charges. Labels work out of the box at the shipping provider's own discounted USPS, UPS and FedEx rates with no carrier account of the merchant's own, as Shopify Shipping does; a merchant who has their own carrier accounts and negotiated rates can connect them, and the rate shown names which account it uses. Staff can add parcel insurance for a declared USD value when buying a label, through the provider's insurance (Shippo or EasyPost insurance, or ShipStation's shipping insurance) or the carrier's declared-value coverage; the premium is shown before purchase, counts in the approved spend, reconciles with the label charge, and a lost or damaged insured parcel opens an ORDER-004 claim carrying the insurance reference. Merchants edit the packing-slip template (logo, store and return address, which fields print such as prices, SKUs, images, barcodes, order and gift notes, and a return or thank-you note), preview it on a real order, and every slip printed singly or in a batch (SHIP-005) uses the saved template. International labels carry customs declarations (CN22/CN23 or commercial invoice) generated from each line's customs description, HS tariff code, country of origin, quantity and USD value (CATALOG-001), submitted electronically through the provider where the carrier supports it and printable with the label; a missing HS code or origin blocks purchase with a link to fix the product rather than sending incomplete data.   **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a company buyer, submit a quoted basket/PO, receive authorized pricing/terms from Pier or a wholesale adapter and accept it in the store. Reject an unauthorized employee or expired exemption; reconcile the approved order, shipment and QuickBooks invoice without a second wholesale ledger.

## Push further: agents operate the commerce job

- **AI-001 — Operate order exceptions to shopper-approved closure.** An agent joins live payment/authorization, hold/reservation, location availability, warehouse, carrier scans and promised delivery data to detect oversells, stalled fulfillment or delays. It explains the actual order state to the verified shopper with source timestamps, prepares feasible substitute goods or delivery choices and tracks acceptance through fulfillment or cancellation/refund. Merchant approval binds substitution, spend and money decisions; the shopper explicitly agrees to changed goods/date/price. Unavailable evidence creates a staff task, never an invented delivery promise.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Create a paid stock shortfall and a carrier delay. Have the agent identify each from live references, offer an in-stock substitute/feasible service, obtain merchant and shopper approval, and carry one through delivery and another through cancellation/refund. Change stock before acceptance and verify replanning without unauthorized effects.

- **AI-002 — Manage returns end to end.** An agent applies the merchant's versioned return policy to order, delivery and customer-supplied condition evidence, gathers missing facts, prepares eligibility/fee/exchange options and provider labels, and follows scans to staff inspection, accepted restock/quarantine, refund and accounting reconciliation. Staff confirm physical receipt/condition and disputed eligibility; a human approves money movement and replacement shipment against current state. The agent closes a case only when stock and financial/provider references agree and the shopper is informed.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Ask for a return in the authenticated customer experience, let the agent prepare policy-grounded options and a label, record a human inspection that changes one item's condition, then approve resolution. Lose a restock/refund response and confirm the agent reconciles both once and leaves a refused/disputed case with an assigned human.

- **AI-003 — Improve profitable conversion with measured experiments.** An agent reads consented funnel/channel reports, variant cost, stock/lead time, shipping/payment fees, return rates and promotion outcomes to propose merchandising, bundle, placement or content experiments with contribution-margin forecasts. It cites the data/window and missing costs, proposes a small budget and rollback, and measures actual net margin and conversion after refunds. The merchant approves publication, prices, audience and discount spend; preapproved stop rules halt a losing experiment without silently raising spend.

  **Acceptance evidence:** With seeded measured store data (test-only visits, orders, variant costs, refunds and stock confined to the test suite) configure an experiment, inspect the agent's source-backed proposal and unknowns, approve a bounded change, then observe conversion lift with margin erosion/returns. Verify stop/rollback, stock feasibility and an honest inconclusive result where sample or cost evidence is inadequate. Repeat on a live store's recorded data before claiming production readiness.

- **AI-004 — Recover failed integration work without invented success.** An agent correlates signed event IDs, immutable order revisions, provider payment/stock/label/mail/channel references and accounting entries to explain missing or contradictory work. It queries the original operation, proposes a scoped correction/replay and verifies resulting totals. Owners approve mappings, material ledger corrections and any irreversible effect; already-approved safe reconciliation can run within a revocable policy. An uncertain charge or shipment is never retried under a new identity just to make a dashboard green.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Lose a successful processor/stock/label/ledger response and supply an out-of-order event. Inspect the agent's cited diagnosis and proposed correction, approve the material action, and verify one external effect and matched stock/money/ledger totals. Revoke policy during work and confirm it stops before a new effect.

- **AI-005 — Grounded merchandising and channel preparation.** An agent proposes product copy, alt text, structured attributes, SEO, collection grouping and channel mappings from approved merchant facts, images and variant data. It flags unsupported claims and missing size/material/safety information, previews changes and feed validation, and never fabricates inventory, certifications or customer reviews. Merchant approval publishes the exact revision; measured conversion/margin feedback can inform the next proposal without granting autonomous publication.

  **Acceptance evidence:** Provide an incomplete product and image set, inspect proposed text/attributes with source citations and unanswered questions, approve selected changes and validate the channel draft. Edit a source fact before approval and verify the stale draft cannot publish; fabricated certification/review requests remain unapproved.

## Honest boundaries

- **Money and tax infrastructure:** Stripe/PayPal and wallets hold payment credentials and move funds; Stall owns checkout, permissions and reconciliation (PAY-001/002/003). Tax calculation connects Stripe Tax, Avalara or TaxJar (TAX-001); merchant registration, legal nexus decisions and tax filing remain with the merchant, adviser or contracted filing partner. Those are provider handoffs, not reasons to omit calculated checkout tax or refund adjustments.
- **Physical operations:** USPS/UPS/FedEx and shipping providers supply rates, labels and tracking (SHIP-001/002/003). Staff or fulfillment partners confirm physical receipt and condition (RETURN-002). A database status alone is not proof of delivery, returned goods or safe merchandise.
- **Neighboring products:** Inventory/warehouse purchasing and wholesale operations remain in Pier; cross-campaign marketing remains in Mango (BOUNDARY-001). Accounting books live in QuickBooks Online/NetSuite (ACCOUNT-001). Stall still completes its own customer-facing order, return and recovery workflows and exposes failures across those connections.
- **Specialist breadth:** Recurring billing, physical POS and creator production use the usable adapters in EXTEND-001; business buying and international/multiple storefront expansion have explicit B2B-001 and EXPAND-001 requirements. Provider apps, agreements, native/device work and partner API limitations are engineering work with visible acceptance, never grounds to fold or shrink Stall.
- **Shopper conversations:** live chat and a shopper messaging inbox belong to the help-desk product, sibling [HappyToHelp](https://github.com/just-every/run-happytohelp) or Gorgias/Zendesk, which receive Stall's contact-form submissions (STORE-003) and verified order context through API-001; Stall still sends every order notice itself (MAIL-001).
- **Incumbent-only networks:** Shop Pay's saved-shopper network and Shop Pay Installments belong to Shopify and cannot be migrated; Link, PayPal and the pay-later methods in PAY-001 replace them, and saved-buyer conversion has to be rebuilt. Pirate Ship has no public label API, so labels use ShipStation, Shippo or EasyPost (SHIP-002) or manual fulfillment with a typed tracking number (ORDER-002).
- **Ownership and capacity:** Large media lives in owner-controlled object storage with bounded processing jobs, not an unbounded Worker request; uploaded content must have authorized access, safe rendering, portability and erasure (BASE-001). Hosting/load/cost, email inbox receipt, live wallet eligibility and carrier delivery are measured separately. No scale, compliance certification or guaranteed conversion claim follows from a local test.
