## STORE-002 — Navigation, collections and product discovery

Shoppers browse menus, manual or rule-based collections and category landing pages, search product names/SKUs/attributes, and filter/sort by price, availability, variant attributes and collection. Results, counts, empty states and shareable URLs agree with published, sellable catalog data; unpublished products stay private. Search offers predictive suggestions as the shopper types, and merchants tune it with synonyms (for example "tee" and "t-shirt"), boosted or pinned products for a query, and a search report of top queries and queries with no results.

Current discovery semantics: `/shop` is the browse and search page and its address holds the whole view (`q`, `collection`, `category`, `tag`, `attr.<name>`, `option.<name>`, `min`, `max`, `available`, `sort`, `page`). Results are the published products Pier lists as sellable, 24 per page, with facet counts that apply every other filter; options, price and in-stock are matched on one variant together. A category or collection page is that address with a collection title and description. Search matches words and word prefixes in names, SKUs (including variants'), categories, tags, attributes, variant options and descriptions, widened by synonym groups (terms stored lowercased without punctuation); a pinned product leads its query and still obeys filters, and a boosted product leads other matches. Suggestions start at two characters and never include a product that is not public. The report keeps only normalized query text with counts, never a person, session or address, for up to 5,000 different queries. Menus have two levels and leave out destinations that are not published. Collections, menus, synonyms and pins are changed only by a signed-in owner or administrator, never an agent. ANALYTICS-001 inherits consented session analytics, STORE-001 theme presentation, and STORE-003 search-engine visibility (sitemaps and structured data).

## CATALOG-001 — Products, variants and media

The desk maintains products, handles, SKUs, variant options/combinations, USD prices and compare-at prices, cost basis, taxable categories, categories/tags/attributes, collections, image galleries and video/alt text. Merchants define typed custom fields (metafields such as size charts, care instructions or ingredients) that themes render on product pages and that imports and exports carry. Products can ask the shopper for personalization (engraving or monogram text with a character limit, a choice list, or a file upload with type and size limits), optionally with a USD price add-on; it is validated before add to cart and carried to the cart, checkout review, order, packing slip, confirmation email and exports, and uploaded files are stored privately under erasure (ERASURE-001). Each variant has independent stock mode and availability (STOCK-001/003), shipping weight/dimensions, and customs description, HS tariff code and country of origin for international labels (SHIP-002). Draft, scheduled, published and archived states preserve historical purchased descriptions/prices and expose only approved current listings.

Current metadata semantics: optional cost is a nonnegative integer number of USD cents; weight is positive whole grams and dimensions are positive whole millimeters (all three together). HS codes accept 6, 8 or 10 digits and origin accepts two uppercase letters; these format checks make no tax, customs or country-validity verification claim. Up to 20 uniquely keyed custom fields carry a text, finite number or boolean value. Omitted details preserve the prior prepared/current facts; an explicit empty details object clears them. Legacy orders without a description snapshot remain unknown rather than borrowing the current listing.

Current listing semantics: A product's page is `/shop/<handle>` (lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens), unique across current and prepared revisions and derived from the SKU when blank. A product has up to three options and up to 100 variants, one per combination. Each variant has its own SKU, unique across products, and may override price, compare-at price, cost, weight, dimensions, HS code, country of origin, customs description and image; a compare-at price must exceed the price it accompanies. Publication requires every variant on sale to be an active Pier item, and each variant's availability is its own Pier item's. Independent stock modes other than Pier-tracked (untracked, store-managed and alternate connectors) are inherited by STOCK-001 and STOCK-003. Images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP) and videos (MP4, WebM) are typed by their content, kept in Stall's database at up to 700 KB each with required alt text, and public only while a published listing references them. Categories (10), tags (30) and attributes (30) are plain text; collections are STORE-002's records, which list a product by SKU or match it by these values. Personalization has up to five fields: text up to 500 characters without control characters, a choice list that accepts only its choices, or a file field that accepts only its selected types (PNG, JPG, PDF), judged by content, up to its limit (at most 700 KB). An add-on price is charged per unit and only the reviewed price is accepted. An uploaded file is private to owners and administrators, attaches to one order, is deleted by customer erasure (ERASURE-001) and, if no order claims it, after 24 hours. A listing is a draft until approved, scheduled while approved with a future publish time, published afterwards and archived when withdrawn; only published listings are public and every order keeps its purchased name, description, variant, personalization and price. When payment is verified Stall makes one plain-text confirmation attempt and records whether it was sent; durable outbox, delivery and bounce evidence and branded templates are inherited by MAIL-001, and carrier packing-slip templates by SHIP-002. The packing slip is a staff-only printable page. Imports carry listing fields through the Stall Details JSON (CATALOG-002 inherits the richer file formats).

## CATALOG-002 — Bulk catalog operations and portable files

Merchants import, export and bulk-edit products/variants, collections, prices, publication, tax settings, stock mode, weight, dimensions, HS codes, countries of origin and custom fields. A dry run shows creations, changes, conflicts and rejected rows before an exact reviewed apply; jobs resume without duplicates and offer undo only where later native edits or orders will be preserved. Original source custody and row-level outcomes remain inspectable.

Current bulk semantics: the native catalog CSV uses Kind, ID and Value columns for complete product and collection records; the legacy Shopify subset keeps its draft-only behavior. The complete grid reuses native product and variant editors. Native apply is an explicit exact-reviewed human catalog approval, replacing any prepared revision; publication and scheduling require current active Pier items for every live variant and the same listing checks as individual approval. Tax categories and customs fields remain merchant-authored facts, not provider verification. Pier-tracked is the currently supported stock mode; STOCK-001 and STOCK-003 retain other stock authorities and CATALOG-003 retains quantity adjustments. Shipping-profile assignments keep their separate SHIP-004 reviewed grid and catalog CSV, remain unchanged by catalog edits and protect products or variants that undo would otherwise remove. Current local authority is checked on every apply, resume, export and undo. Complete before-images, original source bytes and per-row outcomes travel with the journal; undo preserves later native work, purchased variants and retained collection references without changing orders or provider stock.

## CART-001 — Persistent cart and guest or account checkout

Shoppers add/remove variants, change quantities, retain a cart across refresh/sign-in, and check out as guests or account holders. Native shopping/checkout steps collect US addresses, and international addresses in the destination's format for zones the merchant serves (SHIP-001), plus contact, selected delivery method, an optional order note and a gift message with an optional gift receipt that hides prices. They show reviewed USD item, discount, gift-card, shipping, tax and final payable totals before committing payment. Material changes require review again; stock, price and address errors preserve the basket. Saved carts never grant access to another customer. Order notes and gift messages appear on the order, packing slip and confirmation. Merchants can add their own checkout fields (for example "How did you hear about us?", a preferred delivery date or a gift-wrap choice) as required or optional text, choice or date inputs; answers are stored on the order and appear in the order desk, exports and reports (ANALYTICS-001). Current native HTTP cart defaults pending ct approval: a private HttpOnly guest capability or an independently verified account owns each cart. Sign-in handoff is explicit and allowed only to the same verified mailbox, with no foreign-cart or old guest-cookie access afterward. Current Stripe checkout serves US delivery; a Canadian address can be saved but is refused before payment without losing the basket. Review binds the cart revision, published lines, delivery, current checkout questions and exact USD totals. Required merchant questions apply to every existing public Stripe checkout door. Once a checkout starts, immutable original intent and its idempotency identity survive lost replies and later catalog changes; a restored cart carries history without spending authority and needs a fresh copy/review. Shopper note/gift/answer snapshots are separate from internal staff notes and are erased with customer contact. Gift redemption and provider tax are unavailable, labelled explicitly rather than inferred from a zero applied amount.

## PROMO-001 — Discounts, automatic promotions and controlled budgets

Merchants create each Shopify-class discount type as either a code or an automatic discount: percentage or fixed amount off selected products, variants or collections; percentage or fixed amount off the order subtotal; Buy X get Y (buy a quantity of, or spend an amount on, qualifying items and get specified items free or at a percentage/amount off, with a per-order use limit, including buy-one-get-one); and free shipping with a minimum subtotal or quantity, eligible zones/countries and an optional maximum rate. Each has date windows, customer eligibility/segments, minimums, per-customer/total usage, combination rules across product, order and shipping discounts, and budgets. Merchants can bulk-generate unique single-use codes for influencers or affiliates, export them and see redemption per code. Checkout explains applied/refused promotions, allocates discounts to lines for tax and refunds, records the chosen rule revision and emits qualification/redemption/reversal events. Concurrent baskets cannot exceed a shared cap; refund/cancellation treatment is explicit.

Approved policy: CT approved the free-shipping-code default on 22 October: owner and administrator management; reserved and used allocations consume caps; restored rules require fresh activation. The same current-authority and exact-cents boundary governs the added native rules. An unpaid reservation is released only after the provider confirms that its checkout expired or never existed; paid use and refunds retain consumption. Product discounts apply before order discounts, then shipping, with both rules consenting to each combination. Fixed product and order amounts apply once to the selected subtotal; fixed Buy X get Y amounts apply to each reward unit. Reward units never qualify themselves and cheapest eligible reward units are selected first. Each order retains the chosen revisions, exact line allocations and original tax references. These semantics do not waive the original acceptance journey or the shared TAX-001 calculation boundary. End-to-end cancellation remains unproved because the supported Pier connection cannot cancel its reserved order; no stock release or canceled-order success is inferred.

## TAX-001, TAX-002, TAX-003 and TAX-004 — US sales tax and exemptions

Stripe Tax, Avalara AvaTax or TaxJar calculates US state/local sales tax using the merchant's configured registrations, ship-from/destination, product tax categories, exemption evidence and shipping/discount treatment. Checkout shows jurisdictional tax before payment; order records preserve the calculation reference and refunds reverse the correct tax. Provider outage or incomplete configuration blocks a taxed sale with a useful explanation, rather than inventing a zero rate. A sales-tax liability report shows, for any filing period, a state overview (gross, taxable, non-taxable and exempt sales, shipping, tax collected, refunds and adjustments, with marketplace-facilitated channel sales separated), then county, city and special-district breakdowns, then the contributing transactions; each level exports as CSV and drills to orders and provider calculation/transaction references, and refunds land in the period they occur. A nexus view tracks per-state sales and transaction counts against the merchant's configured economic-nexus thresholds and warns before one is crossed. Registration and filing stay with the merchant, adviser or a filing service such as Avalara Returns or TaxJar AutoFile, fed by these exports.

Current implementation semantic: the owner-only HTTP preview door sends complete US destination and ship-from addresses, explicit product tax codes, quantity-extended line amounts and already-discounted shipping to the connected Stripe test account after explicit data-boundary consent. Amounts are exclusive USD integer cents; provider totals and breakdown must reconcile. Active head-office settings, a default tax code and an observed destination-state registration are required. A preview is never a payment, registration, filing or tax transaction. The durable journal keeps no street address; an uncertain calculation is recovered only by retrieving its original reference and matching the full original inputs and line metadata, never by a replacement POST. Restored previews are observation-only and require the original provider calculation. The owner-only consent and restore boundary is approved with riders (ct 25 October 2026). The preview does not enable taxed checkout or substitute for the remaining acceptance journey.

**Approved preview boundary.** Only the current native owner may explicitly consent to and request the current tax-preview data transfer. Consent identifies the connected Stripe Tax account and preview-only purpose; destination and ship-from street, city, state, ZIP and country data; line references, amounts, quantities and product tax codes; discounted shipping; and opaque preview and intent references used for calculation custody. It distinguishes what Stall retains from data sent to and handled by the provider. It grants no payment, tax-transaction commitment, registration, filing or legal-certification authority.

The current preview uses exclusive USD integer cents, quantity-extended line amounts and the supplied already-discounted shipping amount, without applying quantity or shipping discounts twice. It is clearly labelled preview-only. The preview store, history, audit and portable preview records retain minimized opaque custody, attempt and outcome, calculation reference, totals and provider-reported breakdown, without street addresses or raw address-bearing request or response payloads. This preview-retention boundary does not remove the separate order and shipping records required by the commerce contract or claim that Stripe retains no address data.

Restoring a preview restores historical observation and original-reference custody only. It never replays a calculation or restores consent, credentials, sending authority, current quote validity or sale authority. Any receiving-owner connection and new preview use fresh local authority and explicit consent. An uncertain original calculation is recovered only by retrieving and validating its original provider reference against the original intent; neither retry, restart, restore nor a new request identity may replace it with another calculation. If the original reference or matching evidence is unavailable, uncertainty remains visible and actionable. Restored or expired observations cannot be presented as current checkout tax.

No local jurisdiction name or zero rate is invented from incomplete provider evidence. The complete TAX-001 checkout, exemption, order-reference, refund, reporting, CSV, nexus-warning and live test-mode receipt obligations remain unchanged. CART-001, PROMO-001 and ORDER-005 inherit this shared calculation boundary when connected; ORDER-003 and TAX-004 retain correct tax reversals and ACCOUNT-001 retains reconciled posting. These defaults are product decisions, not tax or legal certification.

**Native taxed-sale default (ct approval pending).** Preview consent grants no sale authority. A separate native owner-only, account-bound, explicitly audited consent enables test-mode taxed sales; each shopper explicitly consents to the calculation data transfer. Configuration and exemption evidence alone grant no sending or payment authority. A customer exemption requires an independently verified native customer email and current, unrevoked, state-matching evidence reviewed by the local owner. Restored evidence needs explicit fresh local review.

**Native remedies and marketplace evidence (ct approval pending).** Taxed goodwill needs explicit owner confirmation that it leaves recorded sales tax unchanged, sends no reversal and posts a zero-tax adjustment in its filing period; refunding purchased items or shipping reverses tax. A payment made to a different full address than the reviewed tax address is never fulfilled and no sales tax is recorded for it; the finance-approved remedy refunds the whole payment, tax included, through the same approval and recovery path as any refund. Marketplace-facilitated sales are recorded by the owner with explicit consent as reporting evidence only: no customer, address or payment data, one row per marketplace reference, never replaced once recorded, kept in separate channel rows and totals that are not added to merchant-collected tax, and counted toward configured nexus thresholds. A connected marketplace feed belongs to the channel clauses, which inherit this evidence door.

Checkout consumes a current calculation bound to exact merchandise, quantity, discounted shipping, customer identity, tax configuration and full address. Tax custody retains keyed address and purchase fingerprints and opaque references, while ordinary paid-order shipping records retain their required address. A changed full payment address blocks tax commitment and fulfillment. Provider-refused or uncertain work never becomes zero tax or a replacement calculation or transaction. Explicit recovery retrieves and validates original provider references. Restored sale authority blocks new checkout until a current owner explicitly enables it or keeps it off; historical observation never supplies that authority.

Liability reports use signed original provider tax postings and the selected filing period in the shop time zone, with a separate refund posting when the reversal occurs. Sale jurisdictions come only from provider calculation evidence. Stripe's reversal names none, so refund tax is split over the original provider breakdown by what remains unreversed (a derived allocation, ct approval pending); tax with no named jurisdiction stays visibly unallocated. Configured nexus warnings use the selected explicit period, item gross less refunds posted in that period, and distinct sale-order counts; refunds do not erase completed transactions. Either enabled configured measure can trigger the merchant's warning percentage. No statutory threshold, registration, filing or legal certification is inferred.

## RISK-001 — Fraud indicators and human review

Processor fraud signals, address/payment mismatches and order risk appear in a restricted review queue with their provenance. Merchant-configured rules hold risky fulfillment and notify authorized staff; an explicit release/cancel decision records the reviewer. Scores are advisory and never silently assert fraud, capture funds or deny a refund without the configured authority. The current conservative default restricts configuration and review to human native owners/admins and records one in-app alert per order/signal set. Cancel means cancel fulfillment only: payment and Pier reservations remain recorded for separate authorized financial/stock handling. ct approved these review and cancellation semantics with riders on 23 October 2026: owner and administrator review only, one deduplicated in-app alert per order and signal set, and cancellation limited to fulfillment, with payment and Pier reservations left recorded for separate authorized handling. There is no automatic release, cancellation or refund, by design: only a recorded owner or administrator decision releases or cancels a hold, a fresh provider flag reopens review, and a score never denies a refund. ORDER-004 and future SHIP-002/005/006/007 fulfillment effects inherit the hold gate and explicit handling of money and stock.

## SHIP-001 — Shipping zones, live rates, international USD shipping, pickup and delivery

Merchants configure shipping zones for the US (by state/ZIP) and for Canada and other countries served in USD from the same store, with service eligibility, free/flat/weight-based rules, handling cutoffs and delivery promises; checkout rates packages from current variant weight/dimensions through ShipStation, Shippo or EasyPost for USPS, UPS and FedEx domestic and international services. International rates state that duties and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility unless the merchant enables a supported duties-paid (DDP) landed-cost service through the shipping provider, and checkout collects the destination's address format and the phone number carriers require. Destinations outside configured zones and embargoed destinations are refused before payment. Shoppers choose eligible service, local pickup at a named store location (shown with its address, opening hours, pickup instructions and expected ready time) or local delivery inside the merchant's delivery area (set per location by ZIP codes or a radius, with its own fee, minimum order and delivery instructions) with clear price and instructions; changed address/package invalidates stale quotes. Pickup and local-delivery orders are completed through SHIP-007, never through a carrier label. Each location has a daily handling cutoff, handling days and a business-day and holiday calendar; the delivery date or range promised at checkout is computed from them and the service's transit time, is shown on the product page and in checkout, and the paid order keeps the promise it was sold under. Split-source orders show combined charges and feasible dates. Which rates and services apply to which products is set by shipping profiles (SHIP-004); this clause's zones and rules are the general profile every product starts in. Local currencies and regional storefronts are EXPAND-001. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

## SHIP-002 — Purchase, print and void carrier labels

Authorized staff buy and print outbound/return labels through ShipStation, Shippo or EasyPost, prepare manifests and packing slips, and void unused labels with reconciled charges. Labels work out of the box at the shipping provider's own discounted USPS, UPS and FedEx rates with no carrier account of the merchant's own, as Shopify Shipping does; a merchant who has their own carrier accounts and negotiated rates can connect them, and the rate shown names which account it uses. Staff can add parcel insurance for a declared USD value when buying a label, through the provider's insurance (Shippo or EasyPost insurance, or ShipStation's shipping insurance) or the carrier's declared-value coverage; the premium is shown before purchase, counts in the approved spend, reconciles with the label charge, and a lost or damaged insured parcel opens an ORDER-004 claim carrying the insurance reference. Merchants edit the packing-slip template (logo, store and return address, which fields print such as prices, SKUs, images, barcodes, order and gift notes, and a return or thank-you note), preview it on a real order, and every slip printed singly or in a batch (SHIP-005) uses the saved template. International labels carry customs declarations (CN22/CN23 or commercial invoice) generated from each line's customs description, HS tariff code, country of origin, quantity and USD value (CATALOG-001), submitted electronically through the provider where the carrier supports it and printable with the label; a missing HS code or origin blocks purchase with a link to fix the product rather than sending incomplete data. Label purchase binds the order, package, service and approved spend. An uncertain provider response is looked up by the same reference before another purchase; downloadable label data is access-controlled. Labels are bought one order at a time here or as a reviewed batch of many orders (SHIP-005); a merchant who already ships from their own ShipStation or a 3PL buys labels there instead (SHIP-006). Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

## SHIP-004 — Shipping profiles for specific products

Merchants group products or variants into shipping profiles: a general profile every product starts in, plus custom profiles (at least the 99 Shopify allows) for items such as oversized, fragile, free-shipping or local-only goods. Each profile has its own ship-from locations, zones, rates (free, flat, weight- or price-based, carrier-calculated, with optional surcharge or handling fee) and allowed services, and can be pickup-only or local-delivery-only. Products and variants are assigned to a profile in the product editor, the bulk editor and catalog CSV (CATALOG-001/002), and Shopify shipping profiles map across in migration (MIGRATE-001). For a cart that spans profiles, each offered service is priced as the sum of each profile's rate for its own items in that service; a service is offered only when every profile in the cart allows it; a pickup-only or local-delivery-only item removes shipped services for the order and says which item caused it. A variant whose profile does not serve the destination is refused before payment, naming the line and keeping the rest of the basket. Each quote records the profile revisions it used; editing a profile or reassigning a product invalidates open quotes, and the paid order keeps the profile revision and quoted charge it was sold under.

Current profile semantics: the general profile (`general`, which holds the shop's earlier flat domestic charge as its one `standard` service) and up to 99 custom profiles, each with its own ID, name, ship-from label, ZIP-prefix zones (one to five leading digits; none means every US ZIP), up to 20 services and pickup and local-delivery switches. A service has a code, a name and a rate: free, flat, weight bands (grams, from the variant's weight, else the product's) or price bands (the profile's subtotal including add-ons), plus an optional handling fee once per profile in the basket and a surcharge per item. A rate applies once per profile per basket; a weight or price past the last band means that profile does not offer the service. A product or variant is assigned to a profile by SKU (a variant's own assignment wins over its product's), stored apart from product revisions and effective at once. For a basket, a shipped service is offered only when every profile in it offers that service code, priced as the sum of each profile's rate for its own items; a profile with no shipped service removes shipped services for the basket and the quote names its items; a profile whose zones miss the shopper's ZIP code lists its lines as unserved, and checkout refuses a shipped service before any payment, naming them, while the rest of the basket stays. Pickup and local delivery use the shop's one pickup place and one delivery area (ZIP prefixes, fee, minimum) and are offered when every profile allows them; delivery also needs a ZIP inside the area and the minimum met. Each offered service carries a quote key derived from the destination ZIP, the basket, the used profiles' revisions and the computed charge; checkout with a stale key is refused, and the paid order keeps its service, ZIP, quote key, profile revisions and charge. The ZIP is the one the shopper entered in the shop; Stripe collects the delivery address afterwards (pickup collects none) and Stall does not compare the two. Profiles, places and assignments are changed only by a signed-in owner or administrator, never an agent, are audited, travel in the archive and the records export, and the customer's erasure removes an order's quoted ZIP and quote key. Bulk assignment (the Products grid and a catalog CSV with the headers `SKU,Shipping Profile`) shows every change and applies only the reviewed fingerprint; the catalog CSV is the acceptance's catalog-or-inventory CSV leg, as ct approved on 22 October. Stock-quantity adjustment is CATALOG-003 and STOCK-003. Inherited by later clauses: SHIP-001 (carrier-calculated rates, state zones, per-location pickup and delivery areas, handling cut-offs and promises, address verification), SHIP-002 (labels), SHIP-007 (completing pickup and delivery orders), MIGRATE-001 (mapping Shopify's profiles), CATALOG-002 (the grid and catalog CSV carry the profile column as they widen), CATALOG-003 (the inventory CSV carries the profile column when stock adjustments ship), and every later record kind inherits archive, export, audit and erasure duties.

## SHIP-005 — Bulk label purchase and batch fulfillment

From the order list, authorized staff select paid orders (up to 100 per batch per ship-from location, as Shopify allows) and create shipping labels as one batch. Before review, Stall skips and names every order it cannot label, with the reason: already fulfilled, no shipping required (pickup, local delivery or digital only, which complete through SHIP-007 instead), invalid or unverified address, missing customs HS code or country of origin on an international line, payment not yet captured or verified, a risk hold (RISK-001), or stock not reserved. One review screen shows each remaining order's package preset and weight, default service (from its shipping profile and rules, editable per order or in bulk) and label cost, plus the batch's total spend against the approved limit; one human approval binds the exact batch revision and each order's draft under ORDER-002. Each label is bought under its own idempotent reference; an uncertain or lost provider response is looked up before any retry, so every order ends with exactly one label, and failures are listed for fixing without re-buying succeeded labels. The batch produces one combined label PDF (4×6 thermal or letter) and one combined packing-slip PDF in the same order, plus a pick list, and can be reprinted. Each labeled order moves to fulfilled with carrier and tracking (SHIP-003), dispatches against its stock authority once, and its shopper gets exactly one shipment notice (MAIL-001). Staff can also bulk-mark orders fulfilled with tracking numbers from a CSV upload when labels were bought elsewhere. Batch history keeps who approved, spend, outcomes and skipped orders. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

## SHIP-006 — Merchant shipping apps and 3PLs as fulfillment connections

A merchant who already packs in their own ShipStation account, or ships through a 3PL such as ShipBob or ShipMonk, connects it as the fulfillment connection for a location or for the whole store. Paid orders cleared of payment and risk holds are sent once, with lines, SKUs, weights, customs data, ship-to address, the shopper's selected service and paid shipping, and order and gift notes. A shipment created there (label bought in ShipStation, or a 3PL dispatch) writes carrier, service and tracking back to the Stall order, records the fulfillment, dispatches stock against the line's stock authority once and sends one shipment notice; partial shipments keep the remaining quantities open. Address and line edits and cancellations in Stall propagate to the connection, and an edit or cancellation the external tool has already shipped against is refused in Stall with the shipment named. Orders routed to a connection are not offered for Stall label purchase, so nothing is labeled twice. A 3PL connection may also be the variant's stock authority through STOCK-001's alternate adapter. Duplicate, late and out-of-order callbacks reconcile by the external order and shipment IDs; connection health and orders stuck unsent or unshipped past a set time are visible as staff work. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

## SHIP-007 — Local pickup and local delivery fulfillment

Pickup and local-delivery orders follow their own fulfillment states and never need a carrier, label or tracking number. A pickup order shows in the order list and a pickup queue with its pickup location: staff set the items aside and mark it **ready for pickup**, which sends the shopper one ready-for-pickup notice (MAIL-001: email, plus SMS with consent) carrying the location's address, hours and pickup instructions; when the customer collects, staff mark it **picked up** (optionally recording who collected after an order-number or name check), which fulfills the order. A local-delivery order goes from unfulfilled to **out for delivery**, which sends one out-for-local-delivery notice with the expected window and delivery instructions, then to **delivered**, which fulfills the order and sends one delivered notice; a failed attempt records a reason, returns the order to ready for delivery and creates a staff task. Staff print a pick list for pickup orders and a delivery list for the driver (each stop's name, address, phone, delivery instructions, items and order notes, sorted by ZIP or a route order staff set). Each step is an attributable staff action under the same role checks from the order desk, the pickup and delivery lists (several orders at once) and the MOBILE-002 merchant app. Stock dispatches once against each line's stock authority (Pier, the alternate connector or STOCK-003 store-managed counts) at the pickup or delivery location, when a pickup order is picked up or a delivery order goes out for delivery; a partial pickup keeps the remaining lines open. Repeating a step, a double tap or a stale screen observes the current state and sends no second notice or dispatch. A pickup order not collected within the merchant's pickup window (for example 7 days after ready) becomes a staff task with an optional reminder notice, and staff can cancel and refund it under ORDER-004 with a reason, releasing the set-aside stock. The customer's order view and the merchant's history show each state, who set it and when. Before this capability ships new provider work, it inherits INTEGRATION-001: installation-local secret custody, least privilege, mapping/data-boundary previews, durable original intent/correlation/attempt/outcome and recovery without replacement uncertain effects.

## ORDER-001, ORDER-007, ORDER-008 and ORDER-009 — Current-order mechanics

The merchant desk searches and filters orders by status, customer, date, SKU and channel, and keeps customer/contact/address, original line and total snapshots, payment/stock/fulfillment/return/refund states, notes, tags, assignment and provider references in one history. Staff create manual retail orders, and see overdue work without changing historical truth. Merchant-selected US time zones and USD formatting govern business displays. The shop's named IANA time zone is canonicalized when saved; the initial default is America/Chicago. Desk timestamps use the saved zone and the offset in effect at each event, and “Yesterday” means the preceding calendar date there, including daylight-saving transitions. Stored commercial timestamps and exported timestamp fields remain UTC. Order filters combine: status, customer text, item SKU, a placed-date range and overdue work. A date range is the shop zone's calendar days of placement, both ends inclusive; an order is overdue when it is partly shipped and its latest recorded Pier dispatch (payment time when unstamped) is more than 7 × 24 hours old, a derived label that never changes the order. One history per order lists every audited action with actor and time and the provider references the record keeps. Only a signed-in owner or admin saves the zone; members and agent credentials are refused, the save is audited, and any canonical IANA zone may be saved. Saving a zone rewrites no stored time: labels, “Today” (the zone's current date at each request), “Yesterday” and date filters follow the saved zone from the next request, so an order's placed date can move to another day. A calendar day runs from the first instant of that date in the zone to the first instant of the next, 23 or 25 hours across a transition. Clauses that later add local days, times or date windows (SHIP-001, SHIP-007, PROMO-001/002, RETURN-001, FLOW-001, RECOVERY-001, MAIL-001, ANALYTICS-001, TAX-001, ACCOUNT-001) use this zone unless their own text names another, name it where a shopper sees a time, and resolve a repeated local time to its first occurrence and a skipped one to the first instant after the gap. Customer text matches the order's customer email or recipient name, case-insensitively. Only an owner or admin, or fulfillment/customer-support staff granted ROLE-001 order access, searches. Search covers every order, shows the full match count when the list is truncated (OPS-001), reads no credential, token or checkout request fingerprint, and keeps no separate index, so an erased customer stops matching at once. The channel filter (“Online store”, “Manual”) combines with the others; ORDER-005 and CHANNEL-001/002/004 add their channels when they ship. Notes and tags are internal plain text: a note holds up to 2,000 characters, and an order has at most 50 tags of up to 40 characters. Only a signed-in owner/admin or ROLE-001 fulfillment/customer-support staff adds and reads them; ungranted members and agent credentials are refused. Notes are append-only with author and time, a correction is a new note, and only the owner may redact a note's text with a reason. Each note, tag or redaction is an audited action in the order's history. Notes and tags never reach a shopper or an outside party: no storefront, checkout, notice, receipt, packing slip, customer view, provider or connection payload or API event carries them. They are separate from a shopper's order note or gift message (CART-001). Owner exports and archives include them. Customer erasure replaces each note's text on the erased customer's orders with “Erased with customer contact”, keeping note ID, author and time, and removes those orders' tags and manual payment references; a restored archive keeps them erased (ERASURE-001). Text about a customer on another customer's order is not detected. CT approved this erasure boundary as landed on 22 October: erasing a customer also deletes the notes and tags on their orders; the audit keeps only that notes existed; notes are excluded from customer-facing exports. The ORDER-001/ORDER-006 numeric fallback and the existing PENDING_EFFECT refusal are accepted, with policy unchanged. A manual retail order records a sale already paid outside Stall. A signed-in owner/admin or ROLE-001 finance staff member enters published catalog items at current USD prices and positive whole quantities within checkout's limits, plus the customer's email, name and US shipping address and the shop's configured shipping charge. Members and agent credentials are refused, and creation fails if the server's total differs from the total the staff member reviewed. Its payment is staff-recorded: cash, check, bank transfer or other, for exactly the order total in integer cents, with a received date in the shop zone that is not after today and an optional plain-text reference. It is labelled “Payment recorded by staff, not confirmed by a payment provider” wherever it appears and never counts as a PAY-003 verified payment. Creation reserves stock through the same paid-order Pier reservation, recovery and shortfall handling as a storefront order; shipment approval (ORDER-002) is unchanged. Its refund is a reviewed ORDER-003 refund within the same limits, but the effect is a staff record rather than a processor call: “Refund recorded by staff; Stall moved no money”. It records `no_tax_recorded`, and Stall sends no confirmation or receipt. Orders awaiting payment, custom lines, discounts and invoices remain ORDER-005 and PAY-001, which reuse this recorded payment and its label. TAX-001, MAIL-001, ACCOUNT-001, ANALYTICS-001 and ORDER-004 cover manual orders when they ship.

## ORDER-003 — Reviewed line refunds without duplicate money movement

A human approves the exact revision, purchased lines, reason and computed refund amount. Pending and completed refunds count against refundable quantities/value. Approval persists before provider submission, uncertain responses reconcile using the same provider/idempotency references, and repeat approval cannot pay twice. Besides purchased lines, a reviewed refund can return the shipping charge alone (in full or part, up to the shipping paid), or a goodwill amount with a reason (for example a late parcel) with no line returned, as Shopify's refund screen allows; these count against the order's refundable value, carry their tax treatment (TAX-001) and accounting category (ACCOUNT-001), and never change returned or refundable line quantities. A money refund alone does not assert returned stock or restock an item. Every refund capability that exists today conforms; new payment methods and capture modes (PAY-001/002), tax adjustments (TAX-001) and ledger posting (ACCOUNT-001) inherit exact approval, limits and recovery before those capabilities ship. Current untaxed orders record `no_tax_recorded` and an explicit merchandise, shipping or goodwill category; this does not claim tax calculation or accounting posting. Amounts are positive integer cents, with inclusive limits: pending and succeeded attempts consume the original paid value, shipping refunds additionally consume only original paid shipping, and only item refunds consume purchased quantities. Failed/canceled attempts free their budgets; drafts do not reserve value and are replaced under the order revision. Historical orders without a shipping charge have zero shipping refundable. The human owner/admin or ROLE-001 finance staff member approves within configured limits and approval separation; agent credentials prepare only.

## ORDER-005 — Draft orders, custom lines and payable invoices

For phone, email and commissioned orders, staff build a draft for a new or existing customer with catalog variants, custom line items (title, USD price, quantity, taxable or not, and whether it ships and its weight), line or order discounts with a reason, shipping (rated through SHIP-001/004 or a custom charge) and tax calculated through TAX-001, including customer exemptions; staff may optionally hold stock for the draft (STOCK-002). From the draft staff send an invoice email (MAIL-001) with an optional message and a secure, expiring checkout link. The customer pays through Stall checkout with the PAY-001 methods; if they change the shipping address, tax and shipping are recalculated and shown before payment. A verified payment (PAY-003) converts the draft into exactly one paid order that keeps the draft reference, and the draft then closes. Editing a draft after its invoice was sent creates a new revision and invalidates the old link, which then tells the customer the invoice was updated and pays nothing until a fresh invoice is sent; a second payment through a link whose draft is already paid is refused, and a duplicate payment event reconciles to the same order. Staff can instead record payment by a manual method (PAY-001). The draft list shows each draft's invoice state (not sent, sent, opened, paid, expired), and drafts can be duplicated or deleted without affecting orders.

## RETURN-001 — Customer self-service returns, cancellations and exchanges

An authenticated account or secure guest order link shows order/shipment/refund history and accepts cancellation, return and exchange requests by eligible line/quantity/reason. Merchant policy explains windows, condition, final-sale exceptions, fees, return shipping and available exchange stock. Staff receive review tasks and shoppers see acceptance/refusal and progress; disputed or exceptional decisions require a person. Another shopper cannot access the request or its attachments. Current semantics: the owner publishes one return policy (window in days from the latest recorded dispatch, required condition, a fee shown to shoppers and applied only when staff record the refund, who pays return shipping, final-sale SKUs, exchanges on or off); until it exists only cancellation before fulfillment starts is accepted. A native verified-email account or a private order link opens one paid order, and the server checks every line again for the window, final sale, shipment and quantity not already requested or refunded, refusing an ineligible line with its rule. A return or exchange may carry up to three private photos readable only by that order's customer, a private link for that order and a native owner or administrator. A case is requested, then accepted or refused only by a native owner or administrator person with an explanation the customer reads; no rule, score or agent decides, and a request outside the written policy is refused with its rule and left to the shop to arrange. Accepting a cancellation, a return or a refusal moves no goods, money or order state; accepting an exchange sets one replacement aside under a deterministic Pier command checked against current stock and moves no money. Staff see every case in an in-app list whether or not a workflow rule exists, and the first return or cancellation of an order also writes the FLOW-001 intake record. Customer erasure removes cases and photos. RETURN-002 inherits goods receipt, restocking and the reconciled resolution of an accepted case. ## AI-001 — Operate order exceptions to shopper-approved closure

An agent joins live payment/authorization, hold/reservation, location availability, warehouse, carrier scans and promised delivery data to detect oversells, stalled fulfillment or delays. Merchant approval binds substitution, spend and money decisions; the shopper explicitly agrees to changed goods/date/price. Unavailable evidence creates a staff task, never an invented delivery promise.

## AI-002 — Manage returns end to end

An agent applies the merchant's versioned return policy to order, delivery and customer-supplied condition evidence, gathers missing facts, prepares eligibility/fee/exchange options and provider labels, and follows scans to staff inspection, accepted restock/quarantine, refund and accounting reconciliation. Staff confirm physical receipt/condition and disputed eligibility; a human approves money movement and replacement shipment against current state. The agent closes a case only when stock and financial/provider references agree and the shopper is informed.

## AI-003 — Improve profitable conversion with measured experiments

An agent reads consented funnel/channel reports, variant cost, stock/lead time, shipping/payment fees, return rates and promotion outcomes to propose merchandising, bundle, placement or content experiments with contribution-margin forecasts. It cites the data/window and missing costs, proposes a small budget and rollback, and measures actual net margin and conversion after refunds. The merchant approves publication, prices, audience and discount spend; preapproved stop rules halt a losing experiment without silently raising spend.

## AI-004 — Recover failed integration work without invented success

An agent correlates signed event IDs, immutable order revisions, provider payment/stock/label/mail/channel references and accounting entries to explain missing or contradictory work. Owners approve mappings, material ledger corrections and any irreversible effect; already-approved safe reconciliation can run within a revocable policy. An uncertain charge or shipment is never retried under a new identity just to make a dashboard green.
