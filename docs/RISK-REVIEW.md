# Risk review

Settings → Risk review is restricted to human native owners and administrators. Processor scores and checks are advisory, with the charge ID and provider field kept as provenance; they are never a finding of fraud. Rules default off. Choose which risk levels, failed address/CVC checks and score threshold should hold fulfillment, then save.

Rules apply to a fresh canonical Stripe PaymentIntent and expanded latest charge, retrieved server-side after a verified payment or signed risk callback. Stall validates the payment ID, metadata, currency and amount against the order. Callback-supplied advisory fields cannot replace this read. Enabling or changing rules requires fresh observations before Stripe-paid orders can proceed. Select an existing order and **Read current risk** if necessary. A missing/unavailable observation blocks fulfillment when rules are enabled; it never fabricates a low-risk score.

A hold blocks shipment preparation and approval, including the guarded approval write and the live check before dispatch. Pier still reserves paid stock. An owner/admin explicitly releases or cancels fulfillment against the observation revision. The decision records the reviewer and time in order history. Identical signals do not duplicate alerts or reopen a release. A different flagged signal set can reopen review. Disabling rules does not release existing holds. A completed/approved dispatch cannot be canceled by pretending it never happened.

**Cancel fulfillment** records a fulfillment decision only. Stall moves no money, releases no physical stock and asserts no processor cancellation. The queue states that payment and stock need separate authorized workflows. Existing refund prerequisites, limits, separation and original-operation recovery still apply; risk scores add no financial authority. This cancellation default and owner/admin review policy await ct approval, so RISK-001 remains PARTIAL.

Unread alerts appear in this restricted queue once per order/signal set. Marking an alert read records the staff account. The latest 100 observations/alerts/decisions are shown, with total counts; durable history stays in the database and owner JSON/archive. Risk records contain opaque order/reviewer/provider IDs and whitelisted signals, never customer contact or notes; contact erasure leaves that commercial history intact. No risk credential or provider key is exported.

Provider contract: [Stripe Charge fields](https://docs.stripe.com/api/charges/object), [PaymentIntent retrieval](https://docs.stripe.com/api/payment_intents/retrieve), and [event types](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/types). This implementation reads `outcome.risk_level`, optional `risk_score`, and card address/CVC checks; absent assessments remain absent. Current callback types include signed charge updates, review opened/closed and successful payment intents. Future fulfillment adapters inherit the same hold gate before effects; future MAIL-001 may add delivered staff mail without duplicating current in-app alerts.
